ESAT's Facility Management Solution simplifies complex operations, aids in cost reduction and improves efficiencies.

Overview

Facility Management Solution is designed to help with operating and maintaining commercial/industrial properties. It maintains, manages and tracks assets, properties, facilities and equipment to achieve optimal performance. This solution allows for effective management and tracking of trends for facility usage in centralized applications. Facility Management Solution manages registrations and sorts job requests. It can also help your organization to effectively enhance its reservation system and operations, and to manage and maximize space in the facility by using Auto CAD floor plans.
