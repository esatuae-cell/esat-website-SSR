Catering Management

Catering Management helps manage catering operations, including orders, menus, inventory, staff, costing, billing, and reporting, all in one system.

Overview

Catering Management is a complete solution for managing dining hall and catering operations from planning to final meal distribution. It includes dining hall setup, catering items, recipe management and costing, dinner requests and processing, actual food production, packed meals, catering wastage, and attribute groups. The system helps ensure accurate planning, cost control, efficient production, and smooth catering operations.

Features and benefits

Centralized Catering Management

Manage dining halls, catering items, recipes, requests, production, and wastage in one ERP system.

Standardized Recipes & Costing

Maintain consistent recipes, portions, and accurate recipe costs.

Efficient Meal Planning

Plan production based on approved meal requests and demand.

Accurate Packed Meal Management

Control meal preparation, packing, quantities, and distribution.

Production Control

Compare planned vs. actual production and identify variances.

Cost & Waste Reduction

Monitor food costs and wastage to improve operational efficiency.

Workflow & Approval Control

Digitize dinner requests with clear approval and fulfillment processes.

Better Visibility & Reporting

Provide real-time insights into requests, production, costs, and wastage.
